# Offscript
Bright white surfaces, dark DM Sans typography, violet accents, rounded image treatments and fine interface rules. The visual reference is contemporary creative publishing, especially It's Nice That. There is no cream newspaper palette or serif masthead.

The homepage leads with real stories: one large image-led article and two smaller supporting articles, followed by a quiet archive and topic rail. A compact lowercase `offscript*` masthead and violet dot navigation give the publication its identity. Photography is illustrative and specific to the seeded sample articles; user-supplied covers take priority. Archive rows adapt the 21st.dev Blog Cards component's linked title, separator and description pattern to vanilla HTML.

Mobile reading order: masthead, navigation, lead story, two supporting stories, archive and topics. Permanent article URLs, safe text rendering, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion and all editor/API functionality remain required.
