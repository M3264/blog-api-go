## Platform
web
## Purpose
A generic blog for published stories, guides and updates. Readers browse topics and open permanent article URLs. Editors manage drafts and publication through token-protected tools.
## Constraints
Existing Go API, embedded HTML/CSS/JavaScript, PostgreSQL and Redis. Preserve published content, article slugs, safe text rendering and the Journal identity. No university-specific branding.
## Current task
Rebuild the reader experience in the existing burgundy, cream and serif editorial style. Use 21st.dev components and review against Vercel Web Interface Guidelines. Reader-first emphasis inferred from the prior blog and dedicated article page requests.
