## Platform
web
## Purpose
Offscript is a publication for published stories, guides and updates. Readers browse topics and open permanent article URLs. Editors manage drafts and publication through token-protected tools.
## Constraints
Existing Go API, embedded HTML/CSS/JavaScript, PostgreSQL and Redis. Preserve published content, article slugs, safe text rendering and the Journal identity. No university-specific branding.
## Current task
The reader experience uses a bright contemporary publication style: white surfaces, dark DM Sans typography, violet accents and image-led stories. The name is Offscript. The redesign was guided by It's Nice That and a 21st.dev Blog Cards adaptation. Existing API, content and dedicated article pages remain stable.
